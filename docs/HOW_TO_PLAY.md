# How to play Ripcurrent

## Objective

Ride as many sets as your hearts allow. Tricks, tube time and clean landings feed the score; your best score is saved on this device. Three hearts — a wipeout costs one, and out of hearts the session ends.

## The HUD

- **Score** — tricks, tube time, clean landings, briefings.
- **Hearts** — three chances per session.
- **Speed** — your pace along the face. The wave is always trying to overtake you.

## Reading the wave

The face runs from the **lip** (top, curling) to the **trough** (bottom). Physics is honest:

- **Drop in (down)** — you gain speed. Speed is everything: it buys height for the next carve.
- **Carve up (up)** — you trade speed for position. Carve too much without dropping back and you stall.
- **Stall** — speed below the minimum and the wave closes over you: wipeout.

## Launching and landing

1. Build speed on the lower half of the face.
2. Carve up with momentum — near the lip, press **JUMP**.
3. In the air the board spins; a trick label pops with its value (AIR 180, BIG SPIN...).
4. **Land flat** — board angle near horizontal when you touch down. Steep angle = nose dive = wipeout.
5. Clean landings chain a streak multiplier. Protect it.

## The tube

Periodically the wave curls forward. When it does, get to the **middle band** of the face and stay in the pocket: tube time counts double and the whole screen tints. Leave the pocket and the moment passes — no penalty, no reward. Wipeout during the tube still costs a heart.

## Board briefings

Between sets, while you rest in the calm channel, Kai runs one **board briefing** — a Git-survival concept card (stash vs apply, revert vs reset, what reflog is for). The world pauses while the card is up; correct answers pay +250 and feed the Concepts codex. Skipping is always free.

## Failure and recovery

A wipeout plays out, costs a heart, and the session continues on the next set with full speed reset. Out of hearts: the results board — score, best trick, tube seconds, sets surfed, plus your concepts tally.

## Strategy quick-reference

1. Pump the face: drop, carve, drop — speed is a rhythm, not a number.
2. Do not trick every lip. One clean AIR beats two nose dives.
3. When the sun dips low, the sets get steeper — bank a heart cushion early.
4. The tube is the score engine: 10 seconds in the pocket outearns most trick chains.
